HOW THE ACADEMIC ADVISOR WORKS

Evidence-grounded intelligence powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and official university source documents.

01 INPUT

ASK

Student asks an academic question regarding course prerequisites, attendance policies, fee clearances, or summer registration.

02 RAG SEARCH

RETRIEVE

The engine generates local 384-dim embeddings to search pgvector and retrieve exact verified chunks from official VU source documents.

03 VERIFICATION

VERIFY

University handbook rules, semester spreads, and synthetic student transcript records are evaluated together for eligibility.

04 RESPONSE

RESPOND

The advisor provides a concise answer with official document citations, or requests missing student details when information is required.
